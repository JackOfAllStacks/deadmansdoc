# Core System Specification
- An AI based/assisted system that helps a user create a _personalised set of instructions_ to leave behind for family to help assist them in the case of the users passing
- Records important _knowledge, responsibilities and processes_ that others may need to understand if the user dies or becomes unavailable
- Helps users identify _important information_ they may not think to document themselves
- Organises instructions around _what to do, who to contact and where to find what is needed_
- Allows trusted people to _quickly find and understand_ relevent instructions when they need them
- Connects to or helps users access external services such as password managers and document storage rather than replacing them

## Our System vs. a Will
**Will**
- A **legal document** that records what a person wants to happen after their death.
- Primarily deals with the **distribution of assets, estate, guardianship, and legal wishes**.
- Defines **what should happen and who is legally responsible/entitled**.

**Our System**
- A **practical handover and instruction system** for the people left behind.
- Captures the person's **knowledge, responsibilities, processes, contacts, locations, and instructions**.
- Explains **what needs to be done, how things work, where things are, and who to contact**.
- Covers everyday knowledge that would normally **not belong in a will**.
- Can **reference the will and other external resources** rather than replacing them.

### Simple distinction

> **A will determines what happens to your estate. Our system explains what the people you leave behind need to know and do.**
