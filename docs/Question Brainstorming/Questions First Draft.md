# Questions

## Accounts (phone utilities etc)
- Is anything registered in a business name or an entity rather than your name?
- Which account is the "hub" that others recover through?
- Which services are billed to a card that will be cancelled?
- Which services must stay running for at least six months, and why?
- What are your utilities providers?
- Which services need to **remain active** after your death?
- Are there subscriptions your family may not know about?
- Which services can eventually be cancelled? As well as when?
- Are there any subscriptions that should be cancelled?
- What should happen to your phone number, email accounts etc?
- Are any accounts used to sign into or recover other accounts?
- Are there memberships or services that someone else will need to take over?
- What arrives by post that matters, and how often?
- Do you have a PO box, mail redirection, or post held anywhere?
## Physical Access to things
- Are there valuations, certificates, or receipts for the valuable items? Where?
- Where are the spare keys to your home, car, safe, mailbox, or other locked areas?
- What would would be jepordised to access immediately upon death?
- Where are important things to you physically located that hasn't been written down yet?
- Are there any valuable, sentimental, or culturally specific possessions that require special handling?
- Is there anything in the house that is not yours, and who does it belong to?
- Is there anything stored off\-site — a storage unit, a garage, a relative's shed, a friend's spare room?
- Do you have a safe, lockbox, safety deposit box, or other secured storage?
- Are there important belongings stored somewhere your family might not know about?
- Are any of your belongings currently held by another person or organisation?
- Is there anything that is worth more than it looks?
- Is anything of yours held by an institution — a bank, a gallery, a repairer, a jeweller, a mechanic?
- Who has a key to this house right now?
- Who should be given access to particular belongings/locations?
- Is there anything in your home that your family should throw away or dispose of?
- Is there a security system, and what is the code? Who is the monitoring company?
- Is there anything that would be thrown out as rubbish that shouldn't be?
## Passwords / Credentials
- Which accounts use biometrics — face or fingerprint? \(These die with you\. Anything secured only by biometrics is effectively locked\.\)
- Do you have any logins and passwords not written down anywhere?
- Have you set up anything like Apple Legacy Contact or Google Inactive Account Manager?
- What is the PIN or passcode to your phone, laptop, tablet?
- Do you have a password manager?
- How do you keep your passwords right now?
- Where are recovery codes, backup keys or other authentification methods kept?
- Where is the password written down, if it is written down at all?
- Are there security questions with answers only you would know — mother's maiden name, first pet, first street?
- How do you login or access your bank?
- Do you use the same password in multiple places?
- Which devices are required for 2FA?
- Which device physically receives your two\-factor codes, and what happens if it is locked or the plan is cancelled?
- Is there an account with no recovery path at all?
## **Key People**
- Who are the 3 most important people to contact in event of your death? Why?
- Who makes up your immediate family?
- Is there someone who should **not** be contacted, or not be given access?
- Who would your family instinctively call who would be the wrong person to call?
- Who knows the most about your financial affairs?
- What roles do these people serve in relation to you?
- What are the relationships you have to these people?
- What are the key people's relationships to one another?
- What are their responsibilities before and AFTER your death?
- Have you told these people what you expect of them?
- Who among these people know each other, and who doesn't?
- Who knows something important that even you don't know the detail of?
- Who would disagree with what you've just told me?
### Are there people who know about particular belongings, accounts, or responsibilities?
- Who knows the most about your financial affairs?
- Who could help your family manage things you normally manage?
- Is there anyone who should be contacted about your home or property?
- Who should be contacted regarding your work or business?
- Is there someone you trust to help coordinate everything?
- For each important person: who are they, why should they be contacted and what do they know?
## Finances & Bills
- Are there upcoming payments, debts, contracts, or financial deadlines someone should know about?
- What financial responsibilities do you normally handle for your household?
- How much money do you have in your bank account?
- What payments must continue without interruption?
- What regular bills do you pay?
- Which bills are paid automatically and which require manual payment?
- Where would money be burning immediately upon your death?
- Which bank account normally pays household expenses?
- Do you have a mortgage, rent, loans, credit cards, or other debts?
- Do you have investments, superannuation, shares, or other financial assets?
- Do you receive any regular income other than employment income?
- Are there financial arrangements your family might not know about?
- Does anyone depend on you financially?
- Do you financially support another family member?
- Does anyone owe you money, or do you owe money to anyone privately?
- Are there accounts that **shouldn't be immediately closed** because other payments depend on them?
- Who should your family contact before making major financial changes?
- Where can the relevant financial documents be found?
- Which account do the bills actually come out of, and what happens if it's frozen?
- Which accounts are joint, and which are sole? \(Joint usually survives; sole usually freezes\. Most families discover this the hard way\.\)
- Is there a direct debit that would fail silently rather than loudly?
- Do you have cash, and where?
- Is there anything paid annually that would only surface eleven months later?
- Do you have an accountant or bookkeeper, and what do they do that you don't?
- Which bills would keep being paid for months without anyone noticing?
## Debts, liabilities and guarantees
- Is there a debt that would pass to someone else, or that someone assumes is theirs but isn't?
- What do you owe, and to whom?
- Is there a debt nobody in the family knows about?
- Are you a co\-signer or joint account holder on anything?
- Have you personally guaranteed anything — a business loan, a lease, a family member's mortgage?
- Do you have any tax obligations outstanding or in dispute?
## Money that continues to arrive
- Is there money owed to you that would still arrive after you died?
- Are you owed anything that requires you personally to claim it?
- Does anything pay out annually, seasonally, or on a delay — a distribution, a bonus, a royalty, a dividend, a tax refund?
- Is there income that would arrive but which nobody else knows to expect or chase?
- Does any payment relate to a period *before* the one it arrives in? What would someone misread about it?
- Has anyone borrowed money from you? Is there a record? Would they admit it?
## Home, Pets, Maintenence
- Are there upcoming home or pet\-related appointments someone would need to know about?
- Who currently lives in your home?
- Who do you normally call for plumbing, electrical, gardening, repairs, etc\.?
- Are there warranties or service agreements for anything in the home?
- Do you have anyone/anything depending on you?
- What do you maintain that would go into disrepair upon your death?
- Do you own any property/ies?
- Are there security systems, alarms, gates, smart\-home devices, or other systems someone needs to know how to operate?
### Do you own any pets or animals?
- Who get's them when you die?
- What is each pet's normal feeding and care routine?
- Who is the pet's veterinarian?
- Does the pet take medication or have particular needs?
- Where are pet records, registration and insurance information kept?
## Dependents and care obligations
- Is there a child, grandchild, or relative with particular needs that require ongoing arrangement?
- Are there minor children, and who would be their guardian? Has that person agreed?
- Do you provide care to anyone? What would stop if you did?
- Is anyone financially dependent on you — including someone the rest of the family may not know about?
- Is there someone who relies on you for transport, shopping, medication, or company?
- Do you send money to anyone regularly, here or overseas?
## Urgency
- What has a deadline attached to it that runs from the date of death?
- Can something be lost or otherwise damaged/harmed forever if not done in time?
- Is there a decision someone would have to make within days, and who is qualified to make it?
- What would deteriorate, spoil, die, or be stolen in the first week?
- Is there an order things need to happen after your death?
- What becomes impossible, rather than merely harder, once the institution is notified?
- What urgently needs to happen upon death?
- Is anyone expecting you somewhere in the next month?
- Is there anything currently in progress — a settlement, a claim, a court date, a project with a deadline?
## Health
- How can we plan and sort out your funeral before you die to reduce stress?
- Where are your important health records kept?
- Who is your regular doctor or healthcare provider?
- Are there medical expenses or services that are automatically paid?
- What health and/or life insurance do you have?
- Do you know how you want your funeral to be?
- Do you have the amount of money required for your desired funeral already available\.
- Burial or cremation? If cremation, what happens to the ashes, and who decides?
- Have you consented to donate your body to research or medical training?
- Who is your regular pharmacist? Are there repeat prescriptions to stop?
- Do you have a prepaid funeral plan, a cemetery plot, or an existing arrangement with a funeral director?
- Are you an organ or tissue donor? Does your family know, and would they honour it?
- Are there religious or cultural requirements with a **time constraint** — burial within a set period, particular rites, particular people who must be present?
- Is there a preference about where you die — home, hospital, hospice?
- Are there specialists or clinics with appointments booked?
- Is there medical equipment on loan or rental in the house?
- Is there a family medical history your children should have for their own sake?
## Legal documents and instruments
- Is there any information that may be needed to help your family honor any requests or instructions in your will?
- Do you have an advance care directive or a stated position on treatment?
- Is there anyone you have deliberately left out, or left less to? Do they know?
- Do you have a power of attorney — financial? Medical? Enduring?
- Who is your executor? Do they know? Have they agreed? Who is the backup?
- Who prepared these documents, and are they still practising?
- Where is the **original** — not a copy? Who else holds one?
- Do you have a will? When was it last updated? What has changed in your life since?
- Are there any binding financial agreements, prenuptial agreements, or property settlements from a previous relationship?
- Are there documents you have signed that you don't have a copy of?
- Is there anything in writing that contradicts something else in writing?
- Have you been appointed as someone *else's* executor, attorney, or trustee?
- Do you have a self\-managed fund? Who is the other trustee, and what happens to the fund when you die?
## Superannuation and insurance
- Which super funds are you with — including old ones from previous jobs?
- Do you have insurance held *inside* super — life, TPD, income protection? Many people hold cover they don't know about\.
- Have you ever consolidated funds, or is there money sitting in an account you've forgotten?
- Is there a death benefit nomination in place? Is it **binding** or non\-binding? When was it last renewed? \(Non\-binding lapses; binding usually expires after three years\.\)
- Who is nominated, and is that still who you'd want?
## **Document Specifics**
- Is there any details, language etc that you wish to avoid including in this document?
- Are there specific financial or monetary amounts or values you wish not to be known until after you pass?
